## Results (from the executed notebook; seed 42, CPU; test = 2,000 English sentences sharing no sentence with the training data)

| Model | BLEU | Exact match |
|---|---|---|
| copying the English words (floor) | 0.3 | - |
| encoder-decoder LSTM (256 units, 4.6 million parameters, 12 epochs) | 11.2 | 1.8% |

BLEU by length: 14.4 (1-4 words), 12.0 (5-7), 10.3 (8-10). A random split of the pairs would have put 20.5% of the test pairs' English sentences into the training data.
Training: 30,001 pairs, 12 epochs, about twelve minutes on four CPU cores; the validation loss was still falling (2.45 at epoch 12), so the model is not fully trained.

## Doubts / open points

- **Under-trained baseline:** in an exploratory run that is *not* part of the notebook (batches of 128 and 15 epochs) the score was higher (BLEU 13.8) and the validation loss reached its minimum at epoch 14; the notebook keeps 12 epochs to stay within a CPU-sized budget.
- **BLEU is computed with tokenisation "none"** on lower-cased text split into words and punctuation marks; the numbers are not comparable with published ones.
- **Data licence** is taken from the text inside the data file (CC-BY 2.0 France, Tatoeba); the Tatoeba website was not checked.
- The pretrained-model comparison planned for part 3 may be blocked by the network (a model hub was not reachable from this environment in an earlier project); this will be reported when it is attempted.
